Project Objective

Deliver an elevated, interactive digital gallery showcasing original residency artworks, complete with an immersive walk-through exhibition wall, artist biographies, and a real-time reservation and purchase system.

Exhibition Pages & Functional Scope

Deliverable Scope & Experience Amount (USD)
Page 1: Welcome & Curatorial Page The opening digital entrance introducing the Swahili Futurism movement, coastal residency background, and creative ethos. Designed with custom editorial typography and expressive visual layout. $80.00
Page 2: Catalogue & Virtual Gallery Wall The centerpiece exhibition floor. Features an interactive horizontal gallery wall that allows collectors to walk through and view all residency pieces side-by-side, responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop. $80.00
Page 3: Purchase, Inquiries & Contact Page Dedicated acquisition and contact hub containing buyer instructions, direct gallery and curator contacts, certificate verification notes, and direct invoice/wire payment options. $80.00
Interactive Artwork & Artist Detail System Click-to-view pop-up showcase for each artwork. Displays high-resolution art inspection, featuring dynamic zooming, full artist biography, background story, medium, dimensions, and real-time piece availability. $110.00
Cart Page & 20-Minute Reservation Hold Specialized multi-piece cart built for one-of-a-kind originals. Temporarily holds chosen pieces for 20 minutes with a live countdown and inactivity prompt to prevent simultaneous purchases. $120.00
Instant Payment & Automatic "Sold" Status Sync Online checkout integration that updates the live gallery immediately. The moment a client completes a purchase, the artwork automatically switches to "Sold" across the site with no manual editing needed. $100.00
