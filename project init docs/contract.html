1. Scope of Work & Deliverables

The Developer agrees to design, build, and deploy an exhibition website showcasing 21 original pieces across 9 artists from the Swahili Futurism August 2026 Residency. The project includes the following core pages and features:

  • Page 1 (Welcome & Curatorial Introduction): An opening page introducing the Swahili Futurism movement, coastal residency background, and artistic vision with custom layout and typography.
  • Page 2 (Virtual Gallery Wall & Catalogue): An interactive horizontal gallery wall allowing collectors to walk through the exhibition smoothly across mobile, tablet, and desktop screens, complete with artist filters.
  • Artwork & Artist Detail Windows: Pop-up detail windows for all 21 catalogue pieces, featuring high-resolution artwork inspection, dynamic image zoom, artist biographies, dimensions, media details, and real-time availability.
  • Page 3 (Collector Cart & Temporary Reservation): A specialized cart for one-of-a-kind original artworks that holds selected pieces for 20 minutes with a countdown timer to prevent duplicate purchases or indefinite holds on art pieces.
  • Page 4 (Purchase, Inquiries & Contact): A dedicated checkout and inquiry hub providing direct online purchase links, bank wire instructions, curator contacts, and authenticity details.
  • Pages 5 & 6 (Terms of Sale & Privacy Policy): Clear collector documentation detailing the 70/30 artist-residency split, provenance certificates, insured shipping logistics, and customer privacy terms.
  • Automated Sales & Deployment: Direct payment processing with automatic updates marking artworks as "Sold" immediately upon confirmed payment, complete with live website hosting, security certificates (SSL), and connection to the custom subdomain (arts.zanzalu.org).

Explicitly Out of Scope: Search Engine Optimization (SEO) and rich-snippet indexing, custom ambient sound players, automated digital certificate generation, multilingual language toggles, third-party payment processing fees, and additions beyond the 21 agreed pieces.
